Slovak diacritics — the elimination test

ľ ď ť ô ŕ ĺ ä ž š č

Ľubomír ťažký kôň

Príliš žltúčky kôň úpel ďábelské ódy. Ľúbiť, štvrť, ďalej, výťah, mäkký, vŕba, hĺbka.

Look specifically at ľ ď ť. The caron becomes a raised apostrophe on ascenders in Slovak — if it renders as a floating check mark over the letter, or collides with the ascender, the face was never drawn for this language. Also check that ŕ and ĺ exist at all; they are Slovak-only and frequently missing.
